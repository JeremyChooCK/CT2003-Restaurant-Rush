=================================================================
                 RESTAURANT RUSH - Manager's Shift
=================================================================
You manage a small restaurant for 18 turns with 2 tables, one Host, one Waiter, one Chef and one Cashier.
Customers: Regular (loses 8 per waiting turn), VIP (loses 5, 10% off),
           Critic (loses 12, orders the priciest item, +10 if served on time, else -10).
Happy Hour (once per game, 2 turns): 20% off new orders, waiting customers recover 15 per turn.
Customers choose their own food. You decide what your staff do each turn.

Menu:
  - Salad ($9.00, 1 unit)
  - Burger ($15.00, 1 unit)
  - Pasta ($18.00, 2 units)
  - Salad + Burger Combo ($21.60, 2 units)
  - Salad + Pasta Combo ($24.30, 3 units)
  - Burger + Pasta Combo ($29.70, 3 units)

Objective after turn 18: at least 4 paid customers, revenue at least $45.00, average satisfaction of paid customers at least 60.
Seed: 2003
Start of turn 1: switch on Happy Hour now for turns 1-2? It can be used once per game. (y/n) > y
